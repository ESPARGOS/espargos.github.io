Paper: Passive Sensing with ESPARGOS and Channel Charting

Can passive objects be tracked using Wi-Fi? What are the privacy implications? We made some experiments to figure out if ESPARGOS can localize objects and people based on signals reflected from a ceiling-mounted transmitter.

In summary, ESPARGOS is definitely capable of detecting and tracking passive objects in such a setup. I was honestly surprised by how well it works! That being said, the limited dynamic range restricts the range and thus potential applications (which is arguably a good thing, given the privacy implications). You can read about all the technical details in our research paper.
